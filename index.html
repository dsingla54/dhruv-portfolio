Operations Manager · Flo Mobility

Autonomous Robotics — Bengaluru
Aug 2024 — Present

Early operations hire; sole owner of end-to-end ops spanning procurement, vendor management, and cross-functional execution — at a company building electric autonomous robots that haul construction materials.

// About Flo Mobility

Flo builds battery-powered autonomous robots — the Flo Hauler — that move materials around construction sites, a sector where reliable manual labor is increasingly hard to find. The robots run on a proprietary fleet-management and navigation stack built in-house, offered as a subscription service to builders including large developers and construction firms. Backed by investors such as Mela Ventures and Arali Ventures, the team is scaling deployments across India and pushing into international markets. Joining at this stage meant owning operations end-to-end, with no playbook to inherit.

  • Redesigned core operational workflows from the ground up, cutting average turnaround time by ~80% and scaling execution capacity without proportional headcount growth.
  • Onboarded and managed 50+ vendors — built the evaluation criteria, RFP templates, and negotiation playbooks from scratch, then ran the process end-to-end across procurement cycles.
  • Partnered directly with engineering and product to translate operational pain points into fleet management features shipped to production — effectively operating as an embedded product owner for internal ops tooling.
  • Documented recurring failure points across procurement and field operations into structured requirement briefs, shortening the loop between "problem observed on the ground" and "fix shipped."
  • Acted as the cross-functional bridge across operations, product, and engineering — a hybrid role rare this early in a company's life, and one that put me in the room for decisions well outside a typical ops scope.
