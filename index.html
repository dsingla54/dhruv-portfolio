Flo builds battery-powered autonomous robots — the Flo Hauler — that move materials around construction sites, a sector where reliable manual labor is increasingly hard to find. The robots run on a proprietary fleet-management and navigation stack built in-house, offered as a subscription service to builders including large developers and construction firms.
